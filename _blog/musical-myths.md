This playlist is a collection of Pascu and Rodri's video clips — a duo that takes well-known songs, rewrites the lyrics entirely, and turns them into short stories about characters from different mythologies around the world. It's the kind of content that hooks you because it mixes something familiar (a song you already know) with something you probably didn't (the myth behind it). I put these together because they're the kind of videos you end up replaying just to catch every detail in the lyrics.
