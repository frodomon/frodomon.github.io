story:
  - "I've worn many hats throughout my life. Every stage, every adventure, every venture, every culture I encountered left me lessons that, in hindsight, shaped a flexible and adaptable mindset. I chose to study computer engineering for the range of possibilities it offered — though I never imagined \"designer\" would be one of them. Throughout my career I always kept a close relationship with technology, using data analytics applied to product and service management, regardless of the role I was in."
  - "My path to design wasn't a decision — it was a consequence. And it started in a card shop. During a stint as a freelance front-end developer, my love for Magic: The Gathering led me to start a business: that's how El Tablero de Otto was born. It was while building that hobby store that I started designing without knowing it — finding pain points in other businesses, understanding what different types of users valued, creating experiences that made people want to stay. By the time I realized what I was doing, I was already a designer."
  - "Most of my career has run on project time — secondments, consultancy, squads that assemble for a launch and dissolve after it. I left almost every one of them before the business numbers came in. What each one left me was the judgment I took into the next."
  - "Outside the screens, I keep learning in other ways. I'm a cyclist who has pedaled all the way to Urubamba, a trekker who knows the Pampa de la Quinua, a traveler who takes advantage of every friend's wedding to explore a new region. I'm a Scout by training — and that explains a lot about how I approach problems. Family guides my decisions. Games, music and film are how I process the world when I'm not in designer mode. I think all of that shows up in the work."
